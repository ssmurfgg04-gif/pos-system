See your shop from anywhere

Log in with the admin credentials you set on your till — the same username and password you use in the app.

Your password is verified against a hash your till published to the cloud — the portal can read your synced sales summary, never your products, customers, or receipts. Not activated yet? Open your till's app, change the admin password once (Settings → People & Roles), and this page lights up.

Works with any LedgerPOS shop synced to the team cloud (v1.1.2+).
