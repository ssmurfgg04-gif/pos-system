How to join on the till

  1. Open LedgerPOS on the till. On the sign-in screen, tap “Joining a team? Paste your join link” (or Register, then “Adding a teammate? Use a join link”).
  2. Paste the link into the join link box. It is filled in for you if you opened the link on the till itself.
  3. Type your name and pick a 4-digit PIN (not 0000 or 1234). Tap Join the team. Your shift screen opens and the shop's products sync in the background.

Trouble joining? The link works for 7 days and can be used once. If it expired, ask the owner for a fresh one. If this till was removed from the team earlier, a fresh link still works.
