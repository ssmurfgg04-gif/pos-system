Seven reasons shops keep it

Everything below is in the free download — nothing is locked behind a plan.

01

Selling starts in about a minute

Download (~10 MB), double-click, sign in. The app builds its own database, seeds a starting catalogue, picks a free port and opens itself in your browser. Nothing to configure, nothing to type.

02

Every till finds its team by itself

The till keeps selling offline — sales queue on the machine and sync the moment the network returns. And each new till joins the team automatically from the cloud database: no join codes, no keys, nothing to send. Sell on one, see it on all.

03

M-Pesa the way Kenya pays

STK push lands on the customer's phone; when the line is bad, the cashier types the 10-character receipt code and moves on. Need part cash, part M-Pesa, part card? Split the tender on one sale.

04

KRA-ready books, every month

One click gives the accountant the month's VAT figures — gross, nett, VAT, cash/M-Pesa split — as a CSV that slots straight into the iTax return.

05

Runs on what you already have

Phones work as terminals, USB barcode scanners fire straight into the cart, and receipts print on ESC/POS printers — 58 mm or 80 mm, USB or network.

06

Your books stay yours

The database lives on that machine and backs itself up every day. Nothing leaves the shop unless you turn on team sync — and even then it's your own cloud project. No subscriptions, no vendor account.

07

Free, and yours to rebrand

Open source under the MIT licence. Your shop's name, colours, currency and tax rate live in Settings — resell it to other shops as your own product.
